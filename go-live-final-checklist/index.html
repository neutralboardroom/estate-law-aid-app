Launch philosophy

Open carefully, not broadly.

For the first real paid users, the safest posture is owner-supervised New York/New Jersey launch only. Future states should remain waitlist/inactive until state-specific copy, attorney coverage, fulfillment SOPs, and finalization language are reviewed.

  • Referral rewards apply only to eligible platform fees after approval.
  • Attorney legal fees stay separate from platform fees and referral rewards.
  • AI outputs stay organizational and cannot approve documents.
  • Finalization claims require actual attorney/finalization workflow completion.
