Referral program and QR sharing

Help people start their estate plan or probate organizer free — without turning the referral into legal advice.

Estate Law Aid referral accounts can share a tracked link, QR code, or printable flyer. The user still controls their own account, draft records, uploads, downloads, and optional review requests.

Important: Estate Law Aid is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Referral sharing must not promise legal validity, tax savings, probate avoidance, attorney availability, court approval, or any outcome. Attorney review is optional and only begins after the attorney engagement steps are complete.

Customer-facing message

Start free. Save your work. Decide later if review is needed.

Referred users should hear a simple promise: organize your estate or probate situation, name and save drafts, upload copies only, print/download drafts, and request document-organization help or attorney review only when appropriate.
