Important limits

  • All observations, dates, roles, source labels, and urgency selections are user-entered.
  • The organizer does not determine capacity, exploitation, coercion, undue influence, abuse, authenticity, credibility, legal effect, admissibility, or emergency status.
  • It does not calculate deadlines, report a concern, file a document, contact a professional, transmit data, or create an attorney-client relationship.
  • A compact save stores only counts and general matter labels—not detailed observations, names, sources, account information, or documents.
