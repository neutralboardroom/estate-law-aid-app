Claim, publication, and outreach controls

These deterministic controls demonstrate fail-closed decisions. They do not authenticate staff, verify a claimant, publish automatically, send outreach, activate payments, or change production data.

Verified claim control

  1. Account and email
  2. Identity
  3. Current professional status
  4. Exact-profile authority
  5. Portal specialty
  6. Firm authority where applicable
  7. Staff approval

Email verification or payment alone never grants control.

Controlled publication

Initial publication requires an approved source class, complete canonical record, supported specialty, duplicate review, safe public fields, and recorded human approval.

Automatic-publication gate: Closed.

Manual-first outreach

Outreach is separate from publication and verification. It requires approved professional contact sources, template and batch approval, legal/privacy review, opt-out and do-not-contact checks, frequency limits, and a pause control.

Delivery and automated-outreach gates: Closed.
