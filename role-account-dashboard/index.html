Permission boundary reminders

  • Customers own their work and can name, save, download, and request review.
  • Owner/admin can manage queues, assignments, production gaps, and business status.
  • Staff and paralegals organize information only and do not provide legal advice.
  • Attorneys need conflict, scope, signed engagement, payment/quote, and secure access first.
  • No preview item is marked safe to sign from the dashboard alone.
