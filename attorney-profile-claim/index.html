What must happen before a claim becomes active

  1. Identity and authority review.
  2. Independent credential and current-status review.
  3. Review of material edits and source support.
  4. Acceptance of terms, privacy, and professional boundaries.
  5. Free basic profile control after approval. Billing applies only if an optional paid growth product is voluntarily selected and separately approved.
  6. Separate conflicts, jurisdiction, availability, consent, delivery, and recordkeeping controls before any inquiry eligibility.

Verified claim boundary

Profile control is not granted by an email address or payment.

Smarter Justice must separately verify the account and email, claimant identity, current professional status, authority for the exact attorney or firm page, Estate Law Aid specialty eligibility, firm authority where applicable, and staff approval. Pending claimants may provide evidence and proposed corrections, but cannot publish protected changes or display a claimed or verified state.

Basic claim verification, correction, approved editing, suppression, and removal review remain free.
