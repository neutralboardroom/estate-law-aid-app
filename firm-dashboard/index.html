Profile management checklist

Public facts

Name, firm, office, website, phone, jurisdictions, practice areas, languages, accessibility and consultation preferences.

Claim evidence

Professional email, credential record, firm authority, requested changes, and advertising acknowledgments.

Boundaries

Organic results, paid profile tools, sponsorship, contact requests, conflict review, and attorney engagement stay separate.

Quality

Keep language accurate, plain-English, non-misleading, and free of guarantees, unsupported rankings, or implied endorsement.

Profile quality before publication

Professionals and firms can prepare better public profiles without implying representation.

Public facts

Practice fit, locations, languages, website, contact preferences and profile status should be clear and current.

Claim evidence

Identity, credential and firm authority are reviewed before profile changes are published.

Client boundaries

Profile views, contact requests, consultation discussions and attorney engagement are separate steps.

Profile growth

Enhanced profiles and sponsorship must be labeled and must not secretly change organic results.
