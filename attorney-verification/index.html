Registration and specialty truth

Attorney verification uses separate evidence for separate questions.

Official New York registration facts can help identify an attorney. Estate-law relevance requires its own current source evidence and portal review.

What official OCA data can support

  • Identity and the exact public registration facts observed in the official source.
  • A namespaced internal credential such as NY-OCA-1234567, without calling it a New York bar number.
  • A targeted official-directory recheck before high-trust events such as first attorney publication, claim approval, outreach, featuring, or opportunity eligibility.

What it does not prove

  • Estate-planning, probate, elder-law, guardianship, tax, or other specialty experience.
  • Good standing, absence of discipline, availability, participation, quality, representation, or suitability.
  • A residential address as an appropriate public business contact.

Privacy and correction safeguards

Estate Law Aid publishes only supported business-facing contact information. Residential OCA street addresses are not published. Conflicting identities, uncertain firm relationships, and stale evidence are held rather than guessed.

Each profile includes free correction, suppression, removal, and claim-preparation paths. Public discipline information would require an exact official public order, high-confidence identity matching, and human review. Estate Law Aid does not publish “no discipline” or “clean record” claims.
