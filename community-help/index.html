Community Help

Find free starting points for safety, housing, food, health, benefits, crisis support, and other needs. No account is required, and this page does not collect a narrative or precise location.

If there is immediate danger, call 911. On a monitored device, use a safer device or leave this page when it is safe to do so.

Estate Law Aid focuses on estate and probate preparation. This page provides a safe handoff to broader community support without creating a case or promising dispatch.
