# Estate Law Aid brand assets — v1.1.27

Status: proposed for owner review and integrated reversibly under the v15 release gate because the packaged public logo still said “Estate Help Desk.”

## Design rationale
- Original doorway-and-path mark suggests a clear starting point and guided preparation without resembling a government seal or law-firm crest.
- Navy communicates seriousness; restrained green supports calm guidance; warm gold is limited to a small accent.
- Assets were drawn as original SVG paths for this release. No stock art, copied marks, or external logo assets are used.

## Current assets
- `estate-law-aid-logo.svg` and `.png`: primary light-background header/footer logo.
- `estate-law-aid-mark.svg` and `.png`: compact square mark.
- `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: browser and application icons.
- `source/estate-help-desk-legacy-logo.png`: preserved historical source only; not used publicly.

The proposed mark can be replaced after owner approval without changing route or component architecture.
