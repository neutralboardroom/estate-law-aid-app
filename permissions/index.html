Role permissions

Who Can See and Do What

Production must enforce role permissions so customers, owner/admin, staff, and attorneys see only the right estate information.

Customer

Can ask questions, upload copies, build documents, view saved work, see review status, pay for review, message staff/attorney after workflow allows it.

Owner / Admin

Can view operations, assign staff, review queues, export data, configure AI/payment/workflows, and monitor audit logs.

Staff

Can sort cases, request missing info, review photo/document quality, prepare summaries, and hand off attorney-ready matters.

Attorney

Can review only assigned matters after conflict/engagement controls, add legal review notes, and mark final review status.

Plan. Protect. Prepare.
