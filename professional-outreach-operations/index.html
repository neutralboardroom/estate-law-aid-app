Required operating boundaries

  • Use only expressly published professional business contact information.
  • Owner approval, sender identity, communication rules, and legal review are required before outreach begins.
  • Record opt-outs and avoid duplicate or excessive contact.
  • Do not promise leads, ranking, clients, revenue, endorsement, or outcomes.
  • Do not include confidential user or matter information in outreach notes.
  • Claiming is not required to correct an inaccurate public fact.

Manual-first outreach gate

Preparation is separate from delivery.

  • Use only a reviewed professional business contact source.
  • Exclude suppressed, removed, disputed, opted-out, do-not-contact, deceased, suspended, or identity-risk records.
  • Require approved sender, template, batch, legal/privacy review, frequency cap, bounce handling, complaint pause, and owner authorization.
  • Outreach never verifies identity, grants profile control, activates paid products, or changes organic ordering.
