New York starting point

Check the official small-estate rules before relying on a simplified process.

New York CourtHelp currently describes a small estate, also called voluntary administration, for a person who died with less than $50,000 in personal property. Real property, wrongful-death or personal-injury claims, county practice, and other facts can change the correct path.

  • Confirm whether there is a will and where the person lived.
  • List personal property separately from real estate and possible claims.
  • Gather the death certificate, original will if one exists, family information, debts, and asset records.
  • Check the current county Surrogate’s Court instructions before filing.

New Jersey starting point

Begin with the county Surrogate’s Office.

New Jersey probate and estate-administration steps are handled through county Surrogate’s Offices and related court processes. Use the official state and county resources for the county where the person lived.
