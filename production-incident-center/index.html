Incident playbook

  1. Stop broad intake if payments, uploads, document downloads, or finalization gates are affected.
  2. Keep customers informed using non-legal support language.
  3. Preserve audit logs and redacted exports.
  4. Refund or hold referral rewards when appropriate.
  5. Do not unlock finalization or attorney-review claims during an incident.
