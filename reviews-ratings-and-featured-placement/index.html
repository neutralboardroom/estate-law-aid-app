Directory signals

No fabricated reviews, imported stars, or pay-to-rank ambiguity.

Estate Law Aid currently omits profile ratings, review counts, testimonials, awards, badges, endorsements, sponsored placement, and featured-professional labels because no complete accepted collection, verification, moderation, disclosure, challenge, and correction system is active.

Current state

Signals are disabled

Source websites may display reviews, ratings, testimonials, awards, or badges. Estate Law Aid does not copy those signals into its own profiles and does not convert them into an Estate Law Aid score.

Membership

Payment is not quality

Free factual control and paid membership remain separate. Membership does not prove quality, specialty, credentials, availability, endorsement, or results and does not change organic profile order.

Future safeguards

What would be required before activation

Any future system would require authentic experience, informed consent where applicable, clear sponsorship or incentive disclosures, no professional-authored client voice, no suppression based on negative sentiment, dispute and correction paths, moderation, anti-manipulation controls, source rights, privacy protection, audit, rollback, and legal and owner approval.
