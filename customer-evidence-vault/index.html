Vault rules

Production handling must be strict.

  • Encrypt files at rest and in transit.
  • Log every view, download, share, and delete request.
  • Run malware scanning and OCR/extraction where enabled.
  • Keep original-copy warnings visible.
  • Do not let attorneys view files until the engagement gate permits access.
