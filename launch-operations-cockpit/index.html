Go-live sequence

What must be real before broad paid launch.

This page is designed to prevent accidental over-promising. It keeps the business launch path separate from legal review, court filing, notarization, trust funding, and asset-transfer claims.

01Authentication and rolesCustomers, owner/admin, staff, paralegals, attorneys, and community partners need secure accounts and permissioned dashboards. 02Database and audit logsProduction records, immutable audit events, retention, backups, and permission checks. 03Encrypted uploadsCopies-only consent, encryption key, scanning/OCR plan, access logs, and secure vault controls. 04Billing productsPlatform service prices, checkout, webhook confirmation, refunds, and attorney-fee separation. 05Referral QR and payoutsFirst-party QR, first eligible attribution lock, fraud review, compliance review, payout approval, and reversals. 06AI case navigatorMulti-voice AI with user consent, local fallback, redacted logs, and no legal-advice outputs. 07Attorney networkAttorney onboarding, state coverage, conflict checks, scope, engagement, secure access, and assignment. 08Real-world handoffsNotarization, court filing, trust funding, and asset transfer must be tracked as handoffs, not claimed complete prematurely.
