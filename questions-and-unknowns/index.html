No detailed narrative required

Start with a question, missing fact, missing document, date to verify, or issue to discuss. You do not need to name every person or account.

No silent memory

The working list stays in memory until you explicitly choose “Save on this device.” Nothing is uploaded, sent, or analyzed by external AI.

Preparation, not a conclusion

The export is not legal advice, attorney review, evidence, filing, or a final document. A professional can use it as a discussion guide.

Useful estate-law categories

Goals and family

Assets and ownership

Deeds, account titles, beneficiary forms, business interests, digital assets, debts, and documents to locate.

Which version is current, when it was signed, whether an amendment exists, and what witness, notary, capacity, court, or funding questions to discuss.

Original will location, death certificate, court papers, assets, debts, heirs, fiduciary authority, notices, and dates to verify.
