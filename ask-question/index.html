Possible urgency or review flags

What happens after you ask?

  • Your question is categorized into an estate planning, document update, probate, executor, trust, or family-helper path.
  • Document, asset, beneficiary, fiduciary, and urgency checklists are created.
  • Selected file names, types, and sizes are tracked locally; file contents are not collected.
  • Urgent or complex issues are flagged for attorney review.

Helpful documents to describe

  • Existing will, old will, codicil, draft will
  • Trust, amendment, restatement, schedule of assets
  • Power of attorney, health care proxy, living will, advance directive, HIPAA authorization
  • Deeds, beneficiary forms, insurance or retirement account forms
  • Death certificate, court papers, Surrogate/county probate papers, bank or insurance letters
