Competitive intelligence

Competitor Audit & Feature Inspiration Ledger

This page records competitor-inspired improvements adapted for Estate Law Aid. It is not public marketing copy about competitors; it is a product-planning page for what we adapt, avoid, and do better.

What we learned and adapted

LegalZoom-style bundle clarity

Will package, trust package, powers, health care documents, HIPAA, pet provisions, printed documents, revisions, and attorney-review upsell inspired a clearer package structure.

Adapted: document builder, printable package, attorney review options, revision-ready packet.
Rocket Lawyer-style path navigation

Categories for wills, trusts, powers of attorney, changing plans, executors/heirs, AI-style questions, and legal pro paths.

Adapted: guided path selector, Q&A builder, attorney-review escalation.
FreeWill-style free entry

Free will/health care/POA flow, no-credit-card entry, attorney-fit warnings, printable summary, charitable-gift prompts.

Adapted: free starting point, printable response package, charity/specific gift field, attorney-review flags.
Gentreo-style life milestones

Wills, trusts, POA, health care proxy, pets, digital vault, unlimited updates, and family/life-event framing.

Adapted: life-event update triggers, pet care instructions, digital asset instructions, update-any-time roadmap.
Wealth.com / Vanilla advisor-platform depth

Estate health checks, scenario modeling, AI summaries, document creation, vaults, collaboration with attorneys/CPAs/advisors, and professional deliverables.

Adapted: estate snapshot, scenario-planning roadmap, attorney-review packet, future AI extraction and professional collaboration notes.
ClearEstate-style settlement help

Estate planning plus settlement, probate checklists, executor support, and step-by-step checklists.

Adapted: probate/executor organizer, estate settlement roadmap, court-paper and creditor/debt checklists.

What we should do better

  • Combine planning and probate in one calm front door.
  • Collect assets, beneficiaries, fiduciaries, family facts, and review flags once.
  • Produce printable drafts plus attorney-ready summaries.
  • Keep a free first step without hiding the basic summary behind payment.
  • Use NY/NJ-first state awareness instead of pretending all states are fully supported.
  • Make attorney review an upgrade without implying legal advice before engagement.

What we avoid

  • Fake law-firm claims.
  • Fake secure vault, payment, e-signature, AI extraction, or attorney network claims before configuration.
  • Overpromising probate avoidance, tax savings, Medicaid eligibility, asset protection, or document validity.
  • Generic one-size-fits-all forms with no risk flags.
  • Customer-facing sales-routing language.
Plan. Protect. Prepare.
