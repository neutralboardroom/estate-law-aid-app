Referral reward ledger

Review pending, approved, paid, held, reversed, and denied referral rewards before payout.

This owner/staff preview ledger adapts the referral tracking model to Estate Law Aid while keeping rewards separate from attorney-client engagement, attorney legal fees, court fees, filing fees, taxes, refunds, and chargebacks.

Referral Dashboard
Compliance gate: Do not pay referral rewards until eligibility, payment status, refund/chargeback status, source identity, self-referral checks, professional-responsibility concerns, and tax/accounting handling are reviewed. Attorney review remains a separate engagement-gated service.

Reward rule

Highest eligible platform-service reward for the referred customer, minus rewards already credited or paid for that same customer. Standard preview cap: $50.

  • Free start: $0
  • $49 service: $10
  • $99 service: $25
  • $149+ eligible platform service: $50 cap
