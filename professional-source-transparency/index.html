Professional source transparency

Estate Law Aid publishes neutral, dated profile information to help people prepare for their own verification. A profile is not a recommendation, endorsement, ranking, or statement that a professional is available.

What the source date means

The review date shows when the cited official professional page was checked. It does not prove that every fact remained unchanged afterward.

What remains for the user to verify

Confirm identity, current registration, good standing, discipline history, jurisdiction, conflicts, availability, fees, scope, language, accessibility, and engagement terms directly before relying on a profile.

Official registration check

Use the official New York Courts attorney search. The directory does not substitute for that official check.

Corrections and removals

Professionals and members of the public may use the correction path on each profile. Requests are reviewed before any factual change is published.
