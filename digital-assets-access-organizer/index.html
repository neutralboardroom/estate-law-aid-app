Important limits

  • All labels, people or roles, access/contact paths, instruction locations, sources, follow-ups and statuses are user-entered.
  • The organizer does not log in, recover, close, transfer, value, preserve, delete, or distribute an account or asset.
  • It does not determine ownership, authority, beneficiary rights, legal effect, tax treatment, authenticity, access rights, or deadlines.
  • A compact save stores counts and general matter labels only—not service names, people, access paths, instruction locations, sources, passwords, credentials, documents, or full entries.
  • Full text or JSON exists only when you deliberately download it to a location you control.
